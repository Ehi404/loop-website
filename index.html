Loopy

Real-time camera transformation for your own streams and calls. Pick a character, go live, and send the result to OBS.

Download for Windows
Windows 10 or later. Free to install. Minutes are purchased inside the app.

What it does

Transform your camera

Apply a character to your live camera feed, with lip sync, and use it while you stream.

OBS ready

Open the OBS browser source from inside the app and drop it into your scene.

Minutes, not a subscription

Buy time packs in the app. Unused minutes stay on your account.

Your account

Sign up, verify your email, and keep your characters for the next session.

You are responsible for how you use the output. Accounts that break these rules can be closed.

How to start

  1. Download and install Loopy.
  2. Create an account and enter the code sent to your email.
  3. Add a character, then buy minutes.
  4. Start the transformation and open the OBS browser source.
